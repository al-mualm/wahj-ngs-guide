Wahj ELISA Learning and Analysis Suite

Plain-language rule: if the kit leaflet shows a gently curved standard line, choose the curved option. If the plot looks almost straight across the working range, choose the straight option.

Scope: this page calculates concentrations and basic statistical comparisons for teaching and research use. It does not provide clinical interpretation.

3. Unknown samples

5. Statistical analysis

Choose the study design and compare the groups

The suite will choose the comparison method from the study design you enter. Two independent groups lead to an independent t-test. Two dependent groups lead to a paired t-test. More than two groups lead to ANOVA.

Sample assignment

The chosen design will determine whether the suite uses a t-test or ANOVA.
