Privacy notes

Wahj Al-DNA accounts are required to access the educational lectures and tools.

Profile

Firebase stores your name, email, optional role, country, institution, and email-update preference. This data is protected by Firebase security rules and Google Cloud encryption in transit and at rest.

History

The site stores last opened pages, section IDs, and scroll positions so you can continue reading later.

Email

Email updates are optional. Only users who opt in should be contacted about future site improvements or educational updates. Account data is not sold or used for advertising.
