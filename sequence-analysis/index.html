Student interpretation panel

How to read the evidence without over-claiming

Percent identity

Identity describes how many aligned nucleotides match exactly. Higher identity usually means closer similarity, but it does not automatically prove species identity.

Query coverage

Coverage describes how much of your sequence participates in the match. A high identity with low coverage can still be incomplete evidence.

E-value

The E-value estimates how likely a match of this quality could appear by chance. Lower values support stronger evidence.

Gaps and mismatches

Gaps and mismatches can reflect real divergence, sequencing noise, or poor trimming. They should be reviewed alongside sequence quality.

16S rRNA limitation

Many bacteria share highly similar 16S sequences. A strong 16S hit may support a genus or species group, but it may not be enough to prove a single exact species.

When to review carefully

Low identity, short alignment ranges, low coverage, or many ambiguous bases mean the result should be treated as uncertain and reviewed again.
